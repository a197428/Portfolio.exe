Alexander completed a professional retraining program in 1C development: 256 hours of training, a diploma earned with honors, and the right to implement and support 1C:Enterprise 8 solutions. This is a specific, verified professional retraining in IT; it is not his entire education — completed frontend, JavaScript, and AI courses plus ongoing study are covered separately in the continuous-learning material.

For the final assessment in the 1C Programming program, he built an educational 1C:Enterprise configuration that automates wholesale and retail operations for the fictional Driver auto-parts group.

The configuration includes 11 catalogs, 7 documents, print forms, 2 document journals, information registers for prices and currency rates, 3 accumulation registers for settlements, inventory, and sales, 4 subsystems, and analytical reports. Its code uses procedures, functions, queries, loops, conditions, common modules, object, form, and manager modules, plus client-side and server-side compilation directives. It also implements input validation, age calculation, shared amount calculation, and number-to-words output for print forms.

This demonstrates educational practice in designing an applied accounting system with 1C. It is not presented as a commercial deployment.

Diploma details — number, registration number, issuing organization, city, exact dates, and commission member names — are not published and are not part of the facts.
